# Product

## Register

product

## Users

ParkPilot is designed first for Parking Administrators at CAMT who need to monitor parking operations, diagnose hardware status, approve administrator access, and export reports during day-to-day campus operations. Authorized Parking Users are a secondary audience for public vehicle registration and later LINE-based commuter workflows.

## Product Purpose

ParkPilot is an intelligence layer over the existing CAMT smart parking hardware. It turns camera and edge-device signals into a field operations command center with live occupancy, spatial heatmaps, slot history, system health, reporting, and administrator access management.

## Brand Personality

Operational, calm, precise. The interface should feel like a trustworthy campus infrastructure tool: clear enough for quick decisions, dense enough for real monitoring, and restrained enough for long sessions.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic SaaS dashboards, passive status-board layouts, oversized rounded cards, decorative shadows, gradient or glass effects, floating metric-card grids, and UI that treats status color as decoration instead of meaning.

## Design Principles

- **Map first when space matters.** The parking layout and slot state should be visible before decorative analytics.
- **Status color earns attention.** Green, yellow, red, and blue appear only for operational state, selection, or action.
- **One admin vocabulary.** Navigation, tables, filters, dialogs, controls, and camera tools should feel like one system.
- **Fast scanning beats spectacle.** Use compact labels, predictable alignment, and clear hierarchy over hero-scale typography.
- **Legacy clarity, modern control.** Preserve the old system's direct readability while adding analytics, diagnostics, and access workflows.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text, controls, and status indicators. All interactive controls require visible focus states, keyboard access, and clear labels. Motion should be short, state-based, and disabled or reduced for users with reduced-motion preferences.
